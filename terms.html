Elkomms Community Terms

Version 1 · Published November 18, 2026

Elkomms is a private space for members of Elks lodges. By using it you agree to these community terms. Members accept them in the app before using it.

1. Zero tolerance

Harassment, threats, hate speech, sexual content, content that exploits or endangers children, spam, and anything illegal are not allowed. There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive members.

2. Report and block

Press and hold any message, or open a member's profile, to report it or to block the member. Blocking hides their messages from you and stops them messaging you.

3. What happens to a report

Your lodge's administrators and the Elkomms team review reports within 24 hours. They may remove content and suspend or remove members who break these terms. A report about a private message shares only that one message with the reviewers.

4. Your content

You are responsible for what you post. Don't share anything you don't have the right to share, or other members' private information.

5. Lodge rules still apply

These terms add to your lodge's bylaws and the Order's rules; they don't replace them.

6. Privacy

How Elkomms handles your information is described in the privacy policy. See also our Child Safety Standards.
