Delete an Elkomms account

In Elkomms, open My Profile → Delete Account, enter your current password, type DELETE, and confirm. This deactivates your account and removes your private sign-in credentials, presence, saved items, preferences, device notification tokens, and notification activity.

Your name, profile photo, authored messages and attachments, and creator attribution remain as lodge records. The app describes this before confirmation. To request further removal, or if you cannot sign in, email Elkomms.feedback@liquidrnd.com from the address associated with your account. Include “Elkomms account deletion” in the subject. We will verify your identity and review the request under applicable law and lodge record needs.
