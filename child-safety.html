Elkomms Child Safety Standards

Published November 7, 2026

Elkomms is a private community app for Westchester Elks Lodge members. Child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) are prohibited throughout the app, including profiles, channels, direct messages, attachments, events, and feedback.

Prohibited content and conduct

Members may not create, request, share, or promote child sexual abuse material (CSAM); groom or solicit a minor for sexual purposes; sexually extort or traffic a minor; or use Elkomms to facilitate any other sexual exploitation or abuse of a child. We may remove prohibited content and restrict or deactivate accounts involved in these activities.

Report a concern in the app

Members can open More → Feedback and submit a child-safety concern to the administrator queue. Include the channel or conversation, approximate time, and a description. Do not reproduce or forward suspected CSAM in the report. Administrators review reports and keep child-safety submissions out of the public approved-feedback list.

If the app is inaccessible, contact the designated child-safety point of contact at Elkomms.feedback@liquidrnd.com. If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Response and legal reporting

We review reported concerns, take action on accounts and content when warranted, preserve information as required by law, and report confirmed CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children or the appropriate regional authority as applicable. We cooperate with lawful requests from authorities.

Contact

The designated contact for child-safety and CSAM prevention matters is Elkomms.feedback@liquidrnd.com. See also the Elkomms Privacy Policy.
