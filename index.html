Elkomms Privacy Policy

Last updated November 7, 2026

Elkomms is a private community app for Westchester Elks Lodge members. This policy explains the information the app handles and the choices available to members.

Information we collect

We collect account details including email address, name, authentication information, membership approval and role. Members may add a profile photo, introduction, phone number and contact email. The app stores content members choose to share, including channel and direct messages, attachments, feedback and screenshots, event RSVPs, and saved items.

If enabled, location permission is used for automatic lodge check-in and check-out. The app also stores check-in state and device notification tokens to provide presence and push notifications. Basic service logs may contain device and network information needed to operate and secure the app.

How we use and share information

We use this information to authenticate members, provide lodge features, display member profiles and conversations to authorized members, manage events and feedback, send notifications, and maintain safety and service reliability. Lodge administrators may manage member approvals and roles. Private conversations and restricted channels are available only to authorized participants under server-side access controls.

We use Supabase to host account, database, storage and server functions. Apple and Google may process app distribution and push notifications on their respective platforms. If a member opens Venmo from an event, Venmo handles that payment under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not sell member personal information or use it for advertising.

Security and retention

Connections to the service are encrypted in transit. Access to lodge content requires an approved account, and access to restricted content is checked by the server. We retain information while an account is active and as needed for lodge operations, security, and legal obligations. Retained lodge content is visible only according to app permissions.

Account deactivation and deletion requests

Members can use My Profile → Delete Account in the app. This deactivates the lodge account and removes private sign-in credentials, presence, saved items, preferences, notification tokens and notification activity. To preserve conversation history and lodge records, the member name, profile photo, authored messages and attachments, and creator attribution remain. The app explains this before confirmation. To request further removal or ask about retained data, email Elkomms.feedback@liquidrnd.com. We will review requests under applicable law and lodge record needs. See the account deletion instructions.
